About SIFs

Specialised Investment Funds are SEBI-regulated mutual funds offering long-short strategies for experienced investors. Minimum investment: ₹10 Lakh. No leverage allowed. Naked short positions capped at 25%. SIFs prioritize risk-adjusted returns and downside protection over aggressive growth.
